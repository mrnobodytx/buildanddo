## product_summary

**Product:** BuildAndDo. **Audience:** small-business owners and operators whose
work is scattered across messages, scheduling, customer records and follow-ups.

**Problem:** an operator can see activity in many tools without a reliable account
of what changed, who approved the response, or whether the work succeeded.

**Proposed solution:** turn a sourced business problem into a bounded mission,
record the approved action, have a separate verifier inspect the result, and show
the operator the evidence and remaining uncertainty. Learning is tied to work
the participant can demonstrate.

**Concise pitch:** Learn by doing actual work. BuildAndDo brings the problem,
mission, result and evidence into one inspectable business journey.

The intended judge story is a small business identifying a follow-up problem,
approving one task and inspecting its independently reviewed result. Current
source implements these paths, but the final entry must include an actual
deployed journey on the accepted candidate. No claim is made that every business
operation is automated or that a model verdict is independent verification.

The four Hostinger roles need separate actual proofs: Agents for relevant
research/development work; AI Builder for a meaningful product/UI contribution;
Web Hosting for the public experience; VPS for backend/automation services.
These are intended roles to substantiate, not a statement that this dispatch
observed their use. Do not publish them as proven until their evidence is linked.
