MEET THE MASCOT

Buddi

Buddi is the BuildAndDo block come to life. It turns up where people start, wait or finish something, and like the product it never says a thing is done before it has been measured.

01 · BLOCK
The mark, awake Same square body, red cap and ochre side as the logo, with a face on the paper front.
02 · RULER
Measure first The ticks on its front are a ruler. Buddi measures before it celebrates.
03 · FLAG
A goal on top The small flag is what you are working toward. It comes down while Buddi is building.
04 · MANNER
Friendly, not silly Two dots of cap red for cheeks. Calm voice: no confetti, no hype, no exclamation marks.
POSES AND WHEN TO USE THEM
Calm, on the stage The default. Beside the assistant, in help text and on the dark broadcast stage.
Hello Welcomes, sign-up and the first lesson.
Thinking Measuring, loading, waiting on evidence. Never a success state.
Verified Only after a real check passes: a verified mission or an earned certificate.
Building Empty states that invite work: no missions yet, a new draft.
DO Keep Buddi square, outlined and beside words that say what happened. Use the Verified pose only when the server has confirmed the check. Hold Buddi still. If effects are on, one short hop on arrival is enough.
DON’T Round the corners, add gradients or recolour the cap. Loop an animation, add confetti or put Buddi on every screen. Let Buddi say something is done before it has been measured.
