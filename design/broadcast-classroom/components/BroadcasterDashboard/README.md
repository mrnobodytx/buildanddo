# BroadcasterDashboard

A composed page for the broadcaster: who they are, who is watching and from where, who uses their content, and what agents are doing while no one is watching.

Showcase only, not a bundle export. Order: `BroadcasterCard`, the stage (or its *agents at work* state), `PresenceTimeline`, then `WorldAudience` and `ContentUse` side by side, and `AgentActivity` in the right rail.
