# BuildAndDo judge story

**One sentence:** BuildAndDo watches the important parts of a small business, explains what changed, and turns the next best action into a verified task.

**First customer:** a small-business owner or operator whose work is scattered across messages, scheduling, customer records, follow-ups and manual checks.

**Three use cases:** detect and respond to a missed operational signal; turn a recurring business problem into an approved, bounded mission; produce an evidence-backed daily readback of what happened and what remains unresolved.

**Explicit exclusions for the Day-21 demo:** no claim that every business operation is automated, no unbounded autonomous mutation, no claim that a configured connector is healthy without a fresh readback, and no claim that a model verdict is independent verification.

**30-second narrative:** "A salon owner sees Friday no-shows rising. BuildAndDo records the signal, turns it into a bounded mission, executes one approved follow-up through the authorized worker, then a separate verifier checks the result. The owner sees the receipt, the outcome and what still needs attention instead of another black-box AI answer."
