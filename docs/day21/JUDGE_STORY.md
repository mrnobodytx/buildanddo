# BuildAndDo judge story

**Learn by doing. Verify what you did. Do it together.**

**One sentence:** BuildAndDo is an educational collaboration platform where
people and AI learn by doing real things together: building projects, solving
problems, completing objectives, sharing methods and verifying what happened.

**Participants:** learners, educators, builders, researchers, project teams and
communities, working with people and AI toward an objective.

**Mechanism:** objective → learn → collaborate → plan → do → observe → verify
→ reflect → retain/reuse.

**Core:** classrooms, Field Manual/tutorials, Practice Library, missions,
Evidence Ledger, replay, capability passports and personal progress. Living
Rooms and community channels support collaboration. Capability and practice
states retain their evidence requirements.

**Example domains:** build software, investigate a research question, organize a
community project, create something, prepare a proposal or complete a business
objective. ERP, Firecrawl and n8n are project tools. Business operations is one
demonstration family; revenue/support and Citadel infrastructure support the
platform.

**30-second narrative, to record only with matching evidence:** "Choose
something you want to build or accomplish. Learn a relevant method, work with
people and AI, and turn the objective into one bounded mission. Do the work and
keep the evidence. Inspect what happened with a separate reviewer. Record what
worked, what failed and what changed, then carry that experience into your next
project or a shared practice with its review status intact."

Record one actual journey using the accepted candidate and authorized accounts.
The existing ERP/provider acceptance lane can demonstrate a real project task;
it retains its native receipt, registered provider and independent-review gates.
Show the learner's objective, lesson, collaboration and reflection around that
task. A planned shot, completed lesson, submitted challenge or model answer is
not proof of independent verification, demonstrated capability or economic value.
Unavailable stages stay explicit. This document is presentation guidance, not
evidence that the journey has run.
